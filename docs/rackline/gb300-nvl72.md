# GB300 NVL72 pods in Rackline

Code: `lib/rackline/nvl72.ts` (library, RA sizing, generator), `components/rackline/setup-panel.tsx`
(setup panel docked beside the plan, with live preview), `lib/rackline/setup.ts` (saved answers,
"edited since" fingerprint, auto-feed and cooler helpers). Layout tab → Start a layout → Reference
design; Rack tool "Rack type" for single racks. Tests: `lib/rackline/nvl72.test.ts`,
`lib/rackline/setup.test.ts`. Network systems: `lib/rackline/network.ts` (see below).

## Editing after setup

The result is an ordinary scenario. Its answers are saved on it (`scenario.setup`), so **Edit setup**
reopens the panel with them; a fingerprint shows whether the layout was edited since, and updating
warns that those edits are discarded (adding a new scenario keeps them). Automatic behaviour stays
editable: the generator's rules (network-rack air limit, mechanical load %, duct upsizing, fabric
attach, uplink ratio) are setup options; racks placed by hand take their nearest rack's feeds
(toggle on the Rack tool, change on Power striping); unfed racks, missing CDUs / CRAHs and failing
PDUs are flagged with one-click fixes under Layout → Checks.

The platform is the NVIDIA GB300 NVL72, the basis of the NVIDIA NVL72 AI Factory enterprise RA.
A Vera Rubin (VR200) platform is to follow from its own reference architecture; the Siemens DSX
grid-direct power path stays in the generator for it but is not offered for GB300.

## What the guided setup decides

| Step | Choice points | Drives |
|---|---|---|
| 1 How big? | racks (SUs); 142 kW (RA maximum) or 135 kW (OEM TDP); control-plane rack (12 nodes) | rack kW, switch counts (RA Tables 6–7), control plane |
| 2 Where does it go? | racks per row, two-row pods or single rows, aisles, containment, network rack at end or middle, CDUs in-row or in a gallery, slab or raised floor | geometry, HAC, trays, floor-load check |
| 3 How is it powered? | 2N UPS or 4-to-make-3 distributed redundant; 415 or 480 V; RPP whips or busway; catalog products and ratings | room single line, every rack's A/B feeds, whip / PDU sizing |
| 4 How is it cooled? | CDU product, capacity and redundancy; dry coolers or chillers; coolant supply 25–45 °C; facility ΔT; design ambient; CRAH product | CDU / CRAH counts and placement, rack flow, dry-cooler feasibility, PUE |
| 5 Check and create | bill of materials, checks, sources | create as a new scenario or replace the current one |

    1 BlueField-3 B3240, 1G BMC; 9 NVLink 5 switch trays; 2 SN2201 OOB switches in each rack (DC
    powered); 12 management nodes for the control plane (4 × 200G CX-7 each).
  - *Network logical architecture*: per SU 144 × 400G compute, 36 × 400G converged, 54 × 1G BMC.
    SN5600 (128 × 400G) leaf / spine. Table 6 compute 2/4/8 SU → 8/4, 16/8, 32/12 leaf/spine;
    Table 7 converged → 2/–, 4/2, 7/4; 2 SN2201 per SU.
- **Lenovo Press LP2357** (GB300 NVL72): 135 kW TDP, up to 155 kW peak; 6 or 8 × 33 kW shelves, one
  60 A IEC 60309 whip each (4 whip pairs for 8 shelves); 346–480 V WYE input; ~90 % heat to liquid,

## Modelling assumptions (estimates — verify before issue)

- A GB300 NVL72 is one 40U library item with 8 three-phase power-shelf inputs (4 per feed),
  33 kW each, `phase3: true`. At 142 kW (and 135 kW) one feed's 4 shelves (132 kW) can't carry the
  rack alone; the tool flags it (plan power capping on feed loss, or confirm shelf redundancy).
- The two in-rack SN2201 are library item `sn2201-dc` with no PSUs: their DC power is inside the
  rack rating. OOB cables stay in the rack.
- Loaded rack weight ~1,360 kg (~3,000 lb) is an estimate (Lenovo lists 185 kg for the empty rack).
- Beyond 8 racks: pods of 8 with 6 super-spine switches per pod (the RA is tested to 8 SUs).
- Network racks hold at most 20 kW of switches (air cooled), SN5600 ≈ 2.3 kW, SN2201 ≈ 0.15 kW;
  management node 2U, 1.2 kW.
- Rack coolant flow per kW from Lenovo Table 27 (scaled from 135 kW); dry coolers serve the loop
  while ambient ≤ supply − 3 K (CDU approach) − 7 K (dry cooler approach).
- Mechanical transformer sized at 50 % of IT with chillers, 25 % with dry coolers; parallel
  transformers / generators / UPS are one single-line node rated at the group's N capacity.
- The AI Factory Single-line tool's own GB300 spec (`data/rack-specs.json`, 137 kW, 40 °C supply,
  100 % liquid) comes from a secondary source and differs from the figures above.

## Network systems (Network tab)

Code: `lib/rackline/network.ts`, routing in `cableAnalysis` (`lib/rackline/analysis.ts`), UI in
`components/rackline/network-tab.tsx` and `net-graph.tsx`. Each scenario has network systems
(default: compute fabric, storage / converged, in-band management, OOB), each carrying some cable
types. Every switch is tagged with a system and a tier (leaf / spine). Endpoint cables of a system
run to the nearest of its leaf racks, or spread over every leaf rack ("rail-optimised"); leaf racks
send `uplink` × their endpoint cables to the system's spine racks (1 = non-blocking). The guided
setup sets compute and converged fabrics to spread, OOB to the rack's own SN2201, and sizes each
fiber duct (600×150 up to 1200×200 mm) for the cables it carries. The topology graph has a node
per switch and endpoint device; its edges add up to the Cable pathways schedule.
