## Basis: VR200 modular reference design

Planning-level basis of design (document on file).

| Item | Reference design value | How Rackline uses it |
|---|---|---|
| Deployment unit | 13.44 MW IT = 4 AI Modules × 3.36 MW | default 56 GPU racks = 4 modules |
| AI Module | 14 GPU racks (200 kW nominal) + 16 support racks (35 kW) | `gpuPerModule`, `supportPerModule`, `supportKW` (editable) |
| Layout | per module: compute HAC (2 rows of GPU racks), CDUs, support HAC (2 rows); RPPs at row ends; compute Power Pod above, mechanical Power Pod below | two facing rows per module: GPU racks, CDU slot, support racks; compute and support containment |
| Power Pod | 5 MVA 34.5 kV→480Y/277 V, 5 MW generator + ATS, 6,000 A MSB (85 kA, UL891), 2 × 2,000 kVA UPS, 6,000 A UPS distribution board, 4 × 1,600 A feeders, PF 0.99, ~4.9 MW per Pod | one Pod per module (≥ 2); UPS and generator optional; parallel UPS modelled as one node rated 2 × 2,000 kVA |
| Redundancy | 4-to-make-3 at rack, feeder and Pod level; Pod level "not confirmed" without added capacity | racks on two feeders from two different Pods, rotating through every Pod pair; a "lose one Pod" check reports the worst surviving Pod against the smallest of transformer, UPS, switchboard and feeders |
| Mechanical powertrain | 3.25 MVA transformer, 3.25 MW generator, UPS for CDUs and pumps | one per module; mechanical load is the Cooling tab's estimate |
| Cooling | DLC via rack SFNs and L2L CDUs to dry / fluid coolers 104/122 °F; chillers 68/82.4 °F for CRAHs and assist above 82 °F dry bulb; IT coolant < 45 °C at ~10 K ΔT; TES 31,397 gal (~6 min); N+1 pumps, chillers, CRAHs, CDUs, fluid coolers | dry coolers (or chillers) for liquid, chillers for CRAH air; TES scaled to IT and reported; flow from ΔT (PG25) |
| Planning PUE | 1.27 (17.1 MW site, 2.8 MW mechanical) | shown next to the model's own PUE |

## Rack

- NVIDIA: 72 Rubin GPUs, 36 Vera CPUs, third-generation MGX rack, 45 °C inlet.
- SemiAnalysis: 18 compute trays (4 GPUs, 2 CPUs, 8 ConnectX-9 at 800G, 1 BlueField-4), 9 NVLink 6
  switch trays, fully liquid cooled; 4 × 110 kW power shelves N+1, two 100 A whips each at 415–480 V;
  180–220 kW per rack.
- Power profiles (project basis, editable): **Max-Q 187 kW, Max-P 227 kW**. The rack type carries both
  ratings (`kwMaxQ`, `kwMaxP`); its design load `kw` follows the chosen profile. Set in the setup
  (step 1) or on Rack elevations → Selected device → Power profile.
- Modelled as 8 three-phase inputs of 55 kW, 4 per feed. At Max-P one feed's 220 kW can't carry a
  227 kW rack; the check reports it (the real rack's shelves are N+1, not 2N).
- Ports: 144 × 800G compute, 18 × 800G BlueField-4, 54 × 1G BMC (BMC count assumed as GB300).

## Estimates (verify before issue)

- Network: no NVIDIA Vera Rubin enterprise network RA was available. Non-blocking two-tier leaf–spine
  on Spectrum-X SN6810 (128 × 800G, CPO, liquid), up to 60 rack-facing ports per leaf; switches sit in
  the support racks. SN6810 2U / 3 kW are estimates. 2 SN2201 OOB per GPU rack (as GB300 MGX) and one
  per support rack.
- Support-rack contents beyond the switches are placeholder 2.5 kW blocks up to the rack's design load.
- Rack weight (heavier than GB300's ~3,000 lb) is not published.
- The reference design's values are planning-level: NEC continuous-load application, short-circuit and coordination,
  CFD, hydraulic modelling and TES analysis are still required (the reference design's own validation note). The
  model's switchboards are derated to 80 %, so 6,000 A boards show near full at Max-P.
